## Overview

Too Many Timers is a high-score hunting game made for the [GMTK Game Jam 2026](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-jam-2026) with theme being "Count Down". As a team of two we created a multi-tasking exercise where the player has to stop various different timers such as a simon says timer from reaching 0. Everything including almost all assests was created within the span of 48 hours.

## Highlights

- Unique Timers with different ways of reseting their time
- Multiplier upgrades for more points
- Increasing difficulty over time
- A rubber duck named Steve
